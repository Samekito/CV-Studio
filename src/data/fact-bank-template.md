## 5. Writing style

- UK English spelling unless the candidate asks otherwise. Plain, specific, active voice. First person in cover letters and bios; no "I" in CV bullets.
- Use " - " (spaced hyphen), never em dashes.
- Numbers over adjectives, but only numbers that appear in section 3.
- Lead each bullet with a verb that is true for what the candidate actually did.
- No filler: "passionate", "results-driven", "team player", "leverage", "synergy", "dynamic".
- CV length: fits two pages. Summary 3-5 sentences. Cover letter under 250 words. Platform bios under 1,200 characters unless the platform allows more.
- Mirror the job ad's own keywords where they are true for the candidate - matching systems parse for exact words.
